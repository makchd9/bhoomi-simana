Residences at Simāna

A Home That Understands Space

At Simāna, every residence is shaped by a deeper understanding of how space influences everyday living. Beyond dimensions and layouts, each home is designed to create a sense of ease — where movement feels intuitive, natural light flows effortlessly, and every room offers a moment of calm.

The emphasis is on balance, proportion, and thoughtful planning. Living spaces are crafted to feel expansive yet intimate, functional yet refined. Distinct zoning between private and shared areas allows each home to adapt seamlessly to different moments of the day — from quiet mornings to meaningful gatherings and relaxed evenings.

At Simāna, space is not merely measured; it is experienced. An experience defined by comfort, composure, and enduring elegance.

*Actual Image

Luxury Begins with Volume

With an impressive 11.8 ft floor-to-floor height, Simāna offers a rare sense of openness in the heart of the city. Elevated ceiling heights enhance natural light, improve ventilation, and create interiors that feel expansive, airy, and effortlessly elegant.

This added volume transforms the experience of home — making every space feel brighter, calmer, and more comfortable. A subtle yet defining luxury that elevates everyday living.

Everyday Moments, Elevated

From quiet mornings to unhurried evenings, the residences at Simāna are designed to support life as it unfolds — effortlessly and beautifully. Thoughtful planning, refined interiors, and a sense of openness come together to elevate everyday experiences into moments of lasting comfort.
