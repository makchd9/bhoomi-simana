About Us

A legacy of thoughtful design, refined living, and enduring values

Simāna was conceived on the simple belief that true luxury lies not in excess but in balance. In a city that never slows down, Simāna offers a way of living that feels composed, spacious, and intentional. Every aspect of Simāna, from its master planning to its architectural expression, has been thoughtfully crafted to create homes that endure gracefully and remain relevant for generations.

*Actual Lobby

Limitless Splendor

Perfectly positioned in prime Parel, Simāna combines exceptional connectivity with elevated views that offer a welcome retreat from the pace of the city.

Whether overlooking Mumbai's iconic skyline or stretches of lush greenery, every view is a reminder that true luxury lies in space, serenity, and perspective.

Limitless Perfection

Every residence at Simāna is thoughtfully designed to maximise space, comfort, and functionality. Refined layouts, premium finishes, and meticulous detailing come together to create homes that are as elegant as they are practical.

Expansive windows frame captivating city and sea views, transform everyday living into an experience defined by quiet luxury, openness, and effortless sophistication.

Limitless Stature

Rising gracefully above the city skyline, Simāna’s premium residential towers are designed to leave a lasting architectural impression. With an impressive 11.8 ft floor-to-floor height, every residence is enhanced by abundant natural light, improved ventilation, and a remarkable sense of openness.

Defined by elegant proportions and exceptional craftsmanship, the towers seamlessly balance architectural grandeur with structural excellence, creating homes that feel expansive, refined, and enduringly timeless.

Limitless Serenity

Simāna offers over 54 thoughtfully curated lifestyle amenities spread across its podium level. From wellness and fitness zones to leisure, recreation, and social spaces, every amenity has been designed to enrich everyday living and foster a balanced lifestyle.

Limitless Potential

At Simāna, every residence is envisioned as a space where life unfolds effortlessly. Elegant interiors, thoughtfully planned layouts, and refined finishes come together to create homes that are both beautiful and deeply functional.

Crafted for contemporary living and designed to stand the test of time, these residences offer an exceptional canvas for life's most meaningful moments.

Since 1993, Bhoomi Group has been shaping the aspirations of homebuyers through a legacy defined by trust, quality, and excellence. With 61+ landmark projects completed, over 12.5 million sq. ft. delivered, and 17,000+ families housed across Mumbai, Thane, and Pune, the Group has played a pivotal role in redefining urban living. Every project reflects a commitment to raising the benchmark — not only through state-of-the-art engineering, sustainable practices, and thoughtful design, but also through a deeper understanding of what transforms a house into a home. Guided by a vision that extends beyond buildings and city skylines, Bhoomi Group continues to create lifestyle destinations that enrich everyday living. With an unwavering pursuit of excellence and a passion for perfection, the Group remains committed to building spaces that inspire generations to come.
