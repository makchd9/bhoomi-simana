Life at Simāna

  • THE AMENITIES

    At the heart of Simāna lies a thoughtfully curated ecosystem of over 54 world-class amenities, designed to enrich everyday living. From wellness zones and landscaped greens to social spaces that foster togetherness, every amenity has been crafted to balance energy and calm.

    Whether it is a quiet morning walk, an evening workout, or moments of celebration with loved ones, Simāna offers spaces that evolve with your lifestyle and elevate your daily experiences.

    *Rendered Image of Actual Pool

  • AIKYAM – THE CLUBHOUSE

    Aikyam is the social soul of Simāna — a space where leisure, wellness, and celebration converge. Designed as a vibrant community hub, it brings residents together through thoughtfully planned spaces, indoor games and fitness facilities.

    It is a place where bonds are strengthened, milestones are celebrated, and everyday moments are transformed into lasting memories.

    *Actual Image of Clubhouse

  • THE RESIDENCES

    The residences at Simāna are thoughtfully crafted to bring together comfort, sophistication, and purposeful design. Generous floor-to-floor heights, efficient layouts, and abundant natural light create an atmosphere of openness, serenity, and understated luxury.

    Every home is designed as a sanctuary within the city — a place where thoughtful details, timeless aesthetics, and a sense of belonging come together effortlessly.

    *Actual Image

  • THE VIEW

    At Simāna, the city unfolds before you in all its grandeur. Expansive views offer a constant connection to Mumbai's skyline, creating moments of calm amidst the rhythm of urban life.

    As day turns to dusk, the view becomes a quiet reminder that you are living at a vantage point where the city looks back at you with equal awe.
