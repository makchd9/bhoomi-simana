About Purnata

About Purnata

In the days of yore, our ancestors dreamt of Purnata — a state of harmony and fulfillment where nothing was left to be desired.
Today, that timeless vision finds its true form in Purnata at Simāna by Bhoomi, the final chapter in a landmark address.
In a city where compromise has become a way of life, Bhoomi Group rekindles the pursuit of completeness through spaces crafted for balance, comfort, and connection.
Set in the heart of South Mumbai, Purnata offers seamless access to Worli, the Coastal Road, Sea Link, and Eastern Freeway — placing the best of the city within reach.
Spread across 5.38 acres of freehold land, with luxurious 3 & 4 BHK residences and 80% open spaces, it redefines what it means to live abundantly.
Here, every detail — from architecture to amenities — completes your lifelong pursuit of a life fulfilled.

Project Overview

Experience Infinite Living

Discover the joy of completeness at Purnata — where thoughtful design, spacious interiors, and premium finishes redefine urban living. Every home is crafted to reflect harmony, abundance, and a sense of fulfillment.

Experience Infinite Connectivity

Stay effortlessly connected with prime access to the Coastal Road, Worli, Atal Setu, and Eastern Freeway. Enjoy seamless travel across Mumbai and beyond, making every destination closer to home.

Experience Infinite Comfort

Step into expansive, column-less living spaces with 11.5 ft floor-to-floor height, private decks, and mesmerizing Arabian Sea or city skyline views. Luxury and serenity come together effortlessly.

Experience Infinite Leisure

Indulge in over 54+ state-of-the-art amenities — from a vibrant clubhouse, fitness zones, and pools to an open-air cinema, library, and children’s play areas — designed for every age and mood.

Experience Infinite Harmony

Find spiritual and emotional balance with dedicated spaces like the Jain Temple with Upashraya and serene green landscapes spread across 80% open areas of freehold land.

Experience Infinite Legacy

Built on over 30 years of trust, 50+ delivered projects, and 10 million+ sq. ft. of excellence by Bhoomi Group — Purnata stands as a symbol of timeless quality and enduring value.

About us

Bhoomi group

Since 1993, across 45 landmark projects, 10 million sq.ft of delivered property, and 16,000 housed families across Mumbai, Thane and Pune, Bhoomi Group has shaped an entire generation’s idea of the perfect home.

Every time we manifested a home, we raised the bar. Not just with our consistent state-of-the-art engineering and sustainable practices that elevated the functional and creative elements of a premium Indian home.
But also with our pursuit of finding perfection in the art of turning a house into a home from beyond its four walls.

The road ahead has always been clear for Bhoomi Group. To build lifestyles that transcend homes and cities with a vision for perfection that can only come from within.

Location

Infinite Connectivity

Experience the privilege of living at Simāna, where Mumbai’s most powerful infrastructure networks converge. Enjoy effortless access to Worli, the Coastal Road, the Sea Link, and the Eastern Freeway, placing every part of the city within your reach.
The upcoming Atal Setu (MTHL) connects you swiftly to Navi Mumbai, Pune, and Goa, while the Sewri–Worli Connector ensures smooth travel between the city’s eastern and western corridors.
With every key destination — from business districts and educational institutions to leisure and cultural hubs — just minutes away, Purnata stands at the intersection of South Mumbai’s legacy and the city’s evolving future.
