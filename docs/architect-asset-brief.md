# Simāna — website walkthrough asset request

We are creating an immersive, scroll-controlled website for Simana The Urban Oasis by Bhoomi Properties, Lalbaug. Scope includes all three residential wings, A, B and C. The reference interaction moves from the surrounding city toward the building, explores residences while keeping the building visible, then travels into the entrance and interior.

Please supply either of the following. An existing approved asset is preferable to producing a new asset before we inspect what is available.

## Option A — original 3D scene (preferred for interactive building/floor selection)

- A GLB or GLTF export, including textures. If unavailable, the original Blender, FBX or architectural visualisation scene with its texture files is useful for conversion.
- All three wings in their correct relationship, including the podium, approach and surrounding landscape represented in the approved scene.
- Separate, clearly named wing and floor objects where already available. Please provide the naming convention and floor-to-object mapping. Do not merge them solely for this export if floor selection is intended.
- Existing authored cameras and camera animation paths, if available. Include the entrance/lobby and at least one approved residence interior if the camera is intended to travel inside.
- Scene units, up-axis and any export notes. Identify elements that are conceptual or awaiting approval.
- If interiors live in a separate file, provide their scene and camera entry point. A joined exterior/interior model is not mandatory.

No need to invent missing geometry or remodel the entire project for the initial handoff. A representative existing scene is enough to establish the integration and optimisation work.

## Option B — rendered walkthrough (fastest path to a cinematic scroll journey)

- The highest-quality original MP4/MOV export available; 1920×1080 or higher is preferred. Supply the existing master rather than a WhatsApp/social-media recompression.
- A continuous, slow camera path: neighbourhood/context → all three wings → facade/residence outlook → entrance/lobby → representative residence.
- No burned-in text, logos, music-dependent transitions, title cards or black frames. Existing footage with cuts can still be reviewed, but cuts cannot create a continuous spatial flight.
- Leave short steady holds at the main viewpoints so the website can present project information and residence controls without rushing the visitor.
- A separate portrait render, or generous framing that survives a portrait crop, is helpful for mobile. It is optional for the first handoff.
- Note which shots are rendered visualisations and which are filmed views of the completed property.

A rendered film enables forward/reverse scroll playback along its authored camera path. It does not enable free orbit or accurate selection of arbitrary floors by itself.

## For accurate residence/floor interaction

Please include the approved mapping between wing, floor, apartment identifiers and published floor plans, if available. Current public plan images describe layout types; they do not establish floor-by-floor availability. We will not invent floor counts, unit positions, orientation, dimensions or availability.

## Helpful supporting files

- High-resolution approved elevation render(s).
- Original floor plan images/PDFs with configuration labels and area qualifiers.
- Confirmation that the supplied assets may be used on the project website.

## Delivery

A folder or ZIP containing the model/film and its dependent files is sufficient. Keep original filenames and accompanying notes. The website team will inspect the asset, then optimise it for progressive loading, mobile performance, accessible controls and reduced motion.

## Acceptance target

The building remains the visual centre of the experience. Scroll moves the camera; residence plans appear within that experience. Text, controls and original Simāna branding remain legible and separate from the supplied reference's branding and proprietary assets.
